# Module 8: Your 30-Day Post Plan

**Run time:** 12 to 15 min  
**Outcome:** The firm has a 13-post plan for its first 30 days, the daily habits around it, and a way to review and plan month two.

Film it like a TAB module: workbook on the iPad (or screen-share), you on camera, drawing over the faint guides as you talk. The FILLED PDF shows what each board should look like when you're done.

---

## Slide 1: Cover

## Slide 2: The 30-Day Arc

**Say:**
- Your first 30 days have a shape. Week one, introduce yourself: who you are and who you help.
- Week two, educate: myths, FAQs, rating advice. Week three, prove it: client stories and behind the scenes.
- Week four, convert: how to work with you, what it costs, what the first call looks like. Each week earns the right to the next one.

**Draw:**
- Label each step and the TRUST arrow up the side.

## Slide 3: The 30-Day Map

**Say:**
- Here is the month. Thirteen posts, every Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday.
- Orange is reach, blue is connect, green is convert. Notice we never put a convert post first; we earn it.
- Day 29 you review what worked and book your next shoot day, so month two is already in motion.

**Draw:**
- Dot each post day in its funnel colour. Write REVIEW on day 29.

## Slide 4: Week by Week

**Say:**
- Here is every post. Each one has a topic and a structure from Module 4.
- The full hooks, examples for law and finance, and calls to action are in the separate 30-Day Post Plan document. You can also import the CSV straight into your content calendar sheet.
- Swap topics for your practice area, but keep the order and the mix.

## Slide 5: Notes page

## Slide 6: Daily Non-Negotiables

**Say:**
- Posting is three times a week. These four are every day, and they take about twenty minutes.
- Reply to everything. Comment on five local accounts, especially accountants, brokers and other referral partners. A few casual stories. And log every enquiry that mentions a video.

**Draw:**
- Write one habit per line and tick each box.

## Slide 7: Day 30 Review

**Say:**
- On day 30, put every post on this grid: views along the bottom, enquiries up the side.
- High views and enquiries: double down, film three more like it. Low views but enquiries: great topic, better hook. High views, no enquiries: stronger call to action. Low and low: kill it and move on.
- Paste your numbers into Claude with the weekly review prompt and it will sort them for you.

**Draw:**
- Label the four quadrants.

## Slide 8: Statement: "Done beats perfect. Post all 13, then judge."

**Say:**
- You cannot judge content off three posts. Commit to the 13, then review on day 30.

## Slide 9: Your Plan

**Say:**
- Write your version of each topic, then tick it through script, filmed, edited, posted. Stick it on the wall.

**Draw:**
- Fill in the first three rows.
