# Module 8: Your 30-Day Post Plan

**Run time:** 12 to 15 min  
**Outcome:** The firm has its next 30 days planned: a weekly schedule, a job for every day, a script for every post, and a way to review and plan month two.

Film it like a TAB module on Skool: workbook on the iPad (or screen-share), you on camera, drawing over the boards as you talk. Run the Problems, then the Opportunity, then teach each board. The FILLED PDF shows what each board should look like when you're done.

---

## Slide 1: Cover

## Slide 2: Section: The BIG Problems

## Slide 3: Problem 1: Starting Is Easy. Keeping Going Isn't.

**Say:**
- Most firms post for two weeks, get busy, and stop. Not because content failed, but because nobody planned week three.

## Slide 4: Problem 2: Deciding What to Post Every Day

**Say:**
- Every "what should I post today?" is a decision, and decisions are what busy lawyers skip.

## Slide 5: Problem 3: No Way to Tell What Worked

**Say:**
- Without a review, month two is a guess, and most firms quit before they find what works.

## Slide 6: Section: The OPPORTUNITY

## Slide 7: Opportunity 1: Your Next 30 Days, Already Planned

**Say:**
- Every post is chosen, scripted and dated. You do not decide anything, you just do it.

## Slide 8: Opportunity 2: One Job a Day. Most Take 15 Minutes.

**Say:**
- Post, reply, a couple of stories, log the numbers. About three hours a week.

## Slide 9: Opportunity 3: Review on Day 30, Then Go Again

**Say:**
- Keep what worked, fix what did not, and book next month's shoot day.

## Slide 10: The 30-Day Arc

**Say:**
- Your first 30 days have a shape. Week one, introduce yourself: who you are and who you help.
- Week two, educate: myths, FAQs, rating advice. Week three, prove it: client stories and behind the scenes.
- Week four, convert: how to work with you, what it costs, what the first call looks like. Each week earns the right to the next one.

**Draw:**
- Label each step and the TRUST arrow up the side.

## Slide 11: How This Works

**Say:**
- This is the hand-out you get with this module: the 30-Day Post Plan. Here is how it works.
- Before day one you film all thirteen posts in one shoot day. Every Monday you review last week and schedule this one. Every day has one job. On day 29 you review and go again.
- Client stories: always remove names and anything identifying, get the client's OK, and check your conduct and advertising rules.

## Slide 12: Your Weekly Content Schedule

**Say:**
- Here is your week, the same way I block mine. Monday morning, thirty minutes: Claude reviews last week, then you schedule the three posts.
- Posts go out Tuesday and Thursday at 7:30 in the morning, and Sunday at 7 at night. Block fifteen minutes after each one to reply, because the first hour matters.
- Fifteen minutes at lunch every weekday to reply and comment on five local accounts. Stories on Wednesday, numbers on Friday. About three hours a week.
- Put these in your calendar as recurring events now, like a client meeting.

## Slide 13: Your 30 Days

**Say:**
- And here is the month. Every day has one job and a tick box.
- Thirteen posts: orange is reach, blue is connect, green is convert. We never open with a convert post. We earn it.
- Day 29 is the review and booking next month's shoot. Day 30 is your last post: what it costs to work with you.

## Slide 14: Every Post, Planned

**Say:**
- Every one of the thirteen posts gets a page like this.
- What to say first, the three points to cover, how to end, the on-screen text and a caption you can paste.
- The blue box is a real lawyer video in the same format, with the views it got. Watch it before you film yours.
- Tick it through scripted, filmed, edited, scheduled.

## Slide 15: Day 30 Review

**Say:**
- On day 30, put every post on this grid: views along the bottom, enquiries up the side.
- Views and enquiries: double down, film three more like it. Enquiries but low views: great topic, better hook. Views but no enquiries: a stronger call to action. Neither: kill it and move on.
- Paste your numbers into Claude with the weekly review prompt from Module 7 and it will sort them for you.

## Slide 16: Statement: "Done beats perfect. Post all 13, then judge."

**Say:**
- You cannot judge content off three posts. Commit to the thirteen, then review on day 30.

## Slide 17: Your Plan

**Say:**
- Write your version of each topic, then tick it through script, filmed, edited, posted. Stick it on the wall.

**Draw:**
