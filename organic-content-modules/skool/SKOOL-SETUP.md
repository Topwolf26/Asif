# Skool set-up: Organic Content Program

Built from the workbooks in `pdf/blank`. Set it up like the TAB classroom: each lesson is the video, plus the files listed under **Resources**. Upload the files from the matching folder in `skool/`, they're already named the way they should appear.

## Course

**Title:** Organic Content Program

**Description:** Win more clients with organic content, without it taking over your week. Why it works, what to post, how to film, edit and post it with Claude, and your next 30 days planned for you.

---

## 1.0 Watch This First!

### Introduction 👋

Start here. What this program covers, how each lesson works, and how to get the most out of the workbooks. Print each workbook (or open it on your iPad) and fill it in as you watch.

**Video:** a short welcome, 2 to 3 minutes.  
**Resources:** none.

### Why Organic Content Wins

Why clients now check you out online before they call, and why organic content is the only channel that compounds. Fill in "Your Starting Point" as you watch.

**Video:** Module 1, about 12 to 15 min.  
**Resources:**
- Why Organic Content Wins Wrksht (`skool/1.0 Watch This First!/Why Organic Content Wins Wrksht.pdf`)

### The Content Funnel

The three jobs every post does (reach, connect, convert), the monthly mix, and your own topics mapped to each stage.

**Video:** Module 2, about 12 to 15 min.  
**Resources:**
- The Content Funnel Wrksht (`skool/1.0 Watch This First!/The Content Funnel Wrksht.pdf`)

## 3.0 Make Your Content

- Filming Day Wrksht (`skool/3.0 Make Your Content/Filming Day Wrksht.pdf`)

### Editing with Claude

Hand Claude a folder of raw footage and get back captioned, correctly sized rough cuts the same day. Claude does the slow part, you approve.

**Video:** Module 6, about 15 to 20 min (plus a live demo).

- Editing with Claude Wrksht (`skool/3.0 Make Your Content/Editing with Claude Wrksht.pdf`)
